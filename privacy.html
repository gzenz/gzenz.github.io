Privacy Policy

This application is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to create, list, and delete backup files in the owner's own Drive account.

It does not collect, share, sell, or transmit personal data to third parties.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking are used.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
